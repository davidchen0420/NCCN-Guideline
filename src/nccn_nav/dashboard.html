How to use this navigator

  1. Table of contents (☰): pick a page; section colours are blue algorithm, teal principles, amber discussion. ⌂ shows the whole guideline.
  2. Select answers: click a card to answer at that node. Its pathway is highlighted; other paths hide until you switch on Non-active paths. ↺ clears every answer.
  3. Footnotes: click a letter badge on a card, or open ✱ at the bottom, to read it and step through every place it applies ("1 of N instances").
  4. E and P at the bottom explain evidence categories and preference tags.
  5. Search (⌕): results appear in this panel; use quotes for an exact match; filters gray out content that does not apply.
  6. Zoom: wheel or the slider; ◎ fits the page; ⤡ zooms out to all pages. Back/forward follow your path through pages.
  7. Patient facts (right panel): enter what you know and run a walk; conditions the facts decide are followed automatically.

FAQ

Why does a walk stop?The box has no encoded condition or a needed fact is blank. Choose the branch yourself.
Are branch conditions clinician-reviewed?No. They are machine-drafted and flagged as drafts. Always check the source guideline.
Is this the official NCCN tool?No. It is an independent research prototype built from public descriptions of the Navigator. For clinical decisions use the official NCCN Guidelines.
