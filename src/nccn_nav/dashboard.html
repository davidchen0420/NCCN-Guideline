How to use this navigator

  1. Move around: pick a page in the left list (colour = section: blue algorithm, teal principles, amber discussion) or use search.
  2. Select answers: click a box to answer at that node. The pathway through it is highlighted and everything else dims. Click again to unselect; Clear resets all answers.
  3. Footnotes: hover a box to preview its footnotes; open the Details tab to read them and highlight every box that uses one.
  4. Linked pages: boxes that say "See PROS-x" jump to that page; Back and Forward retrace your steps.
  5. Zoom: scroll or use + and −; Whole guideline zooms out to a map of all pages.
  6. Patient facts: enter what you know and run a walk; conditions that the facts decide are followed automatically.

FAQ

Why does a walk stop?The box has no encoded condition, or a needed fact is blank. Choose the branch yourself.
Are the branch conditions clinician-reviewed?No. They are machine-drafted from the guideline and flagged as drafts. Always check the source guideline.
What do the arrow styles mean?Turn on "show extraction confidence": dashed blue arrows were confirmed by image review, dotted red ones are uncertain.
Is this the official NCCN tool?No. It is an independent research prototype. For clinical decisions use the official NCCN Guidelines.
