A page is only a real miss after reading it; the last column is that decision.

Review notes. `genuine` in the worklist is a heuristic (vector drawings and body text of the PDF page); the `review` column holds decisions made by reading the page where one was made. How to work through the worklist: docs/reextract-pages.md.

- Genuine footnote pages exist too (PROS-10A, PROS-9A, the ESOPH/GAST/LCS/ME/ICI footnote pages): they are not re-extracted, and the audit counts them under "Not listed".
- Two pages with one printed code lose the first page's boxes (WILMS-5 / WILMS-11, AFAP-1, GALL-4, AYAO-8): fixed for WILMS, listed for the rest.
